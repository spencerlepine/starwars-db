# Sound effects (reference + usable in the game)

Everything here is CC0 (public domain) and safe to ship in the game:

- `kenney-sci-fi/` (73): Kenney "Sci-Fi Sounds": lasers, engines, explosions,
  doors, force fields, computer noise, thrusters.
- `kenney-digital/` (62): Kenney "Digital Audio": arcade lasers, phasers,
  zaps, blips, power-ups.
- `synth/` (17): original sounds generated by `tools/synth_sfx.py` to evoke
  the saga's signature sounds that free packs don't cover: lightsaber
  hum/ignite/swing/clash/retract, guy-wire blaster bolts, astromech chatter,
  hyperspace jump, TIE-style fly-by howl. Tweak parameters and re-run to make
  more variants.

`data/sfx.csv` describes every file (category, the Star Wars sound it stands in
for, `essential` flag, source, license); `data/sfx_entities.csv` links sounds
to starwars-db entities. `tools/build_sfx_manifest.py` rebuilds both, and
`tools/export_sfx_db.py` publishes them to starwars-db.

The real film sound effects (Ben Burtt's originals) are copyrighted by
Lucasfilm, so they are deliberately not collected here.
